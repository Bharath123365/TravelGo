Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your questions about TravelGo.

1. How can I search for buses?

Open the homepage, enter your source city, destination city and journey date, then click Search Buses.

2. Can I select my preferred seat?

Yes. Select an available seat from the seat selection page and continue with passenger details.

3. How can I book a ticket?

Search for a bus, select seats, enter passenger details and complete the demo booking process.

4. How can I download my ticket?

After booking, open the confirmation page and click the Download or Print Ticket option.

5. Where can I see my previous bookings?

Click My Bookings in the navigation menu to view your saved booking history.

6. Does TravelGo support online payments?

Currently, TravelGo is a frontend demo project. It does not process real payments.

7. Are the bus timings and prices real?

No. The current bus timings, prices and availability are sample information used for demonstration.

8. Can I use TravelGo on mobile?

Yes. The website has responsive design for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.
