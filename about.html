About TravelGo

Your Journey, Our Responsibility.

Who We Are

TravelGo is a simple online bus booking platform designed to make journey planning easier. Users can search for buses, select seats, enter passenger details and generate a digital ticket.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a simple and user-friendly bus booking experience with easy navigation and convenient booking management.

Why Choose TravelGo?

  • Simple bus search
  • Easy seat selection
  • Digital ticket generation
  • Booking history management
  • Mobile-friendly website

TravelGo is currently a frontend demonstration project. Bus details and bookings are sample data stored in your browser.
